Vol. XIV · No. 7 Blackmoor Parish · 12 October 1978 Price 8p

Third disappearance linked to Victorian mansion on Hollow Road

From our parish correspondent

Constables continue to search the grounds of the abandoned Victorian mansion on Hollow Road after a third resident of Blackmoor Parish was reported missing this autumn. Neighbours speak of lights in the upper windows and a melody heard after midnight, though the house has stood empty since the Blackmoor family left in ’61.

Olivia H., a private researcher recently seen photocopying parish records, told this paper she believed the vanishings were connected. “Someone has been living in the lower rooms,” she said. “The public ledger only prints what it is allowed to print.”

Parish clerks confirm Miss H. requested microfilm of prior editions concerning Hollow Road. Those reels are held under the paper’s own name in the parish archive cabinet — ask for the title that once crowned this page.

Anyone with information is asked to write to the editor. Further notices will appear in subsequent numbers of this journal.
